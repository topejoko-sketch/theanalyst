Case studies

Three healthcare consulting projects completed in 2026 as part of a business analysis team. Each was a team project built on a realistic case scenario. Client names are withheld, my own contribution is stated separately from team deliverables, and all figures are case estimates that would need validation before use.

Team consulting project, 2026, regional hospital network

Closed-loop medication and pharmacy optimisation for a regional hospital network

The problem
A network of 12 hospitals and 45 outpatient clinics ran prescribing, pharmacy inventory and bedside administration on three disconnected systems. The result was medication safety risk, stock-outs, wastage and heavy nursing time spent on manual checks. The avoidable impact was modelled at £2.1M a year (planning estimate).
My contribution
I identified and analysed the stakeholders, planned stakeholder engagement and communication, and helped rank influence, interest and engagement priorities using a Power/Interest matrix. I also contributed to the financial evaluation, including return on investment, payback period and net present value.
Team deliverables
Five Case Model business case, options appraisal, SWOT, cost-benefit analysis, risk register, a 15-stakeholder register with RACI, and a stage-gated Hybrid Agile delivery approach.
Outcome
The team recommended an integrated closed-loop medication management platform with success criteria, governance structures, a risk framework and an implementation roadmap. The model projected £1.92M in annual net operating benefits against a £1.2M one-off cost, a 7.5-month payback and a five-year NPV of about £7.11M, subject to validation.

Team consulting project, 2026, residential care home

Electronic medication administration record for a residential care home

The problem
Medication ordering, controlled-drug double sign-off, round assignment, stock checks and MAR charts were all manual or paper-based. That added workload for care staff and raised the risk of errors.
My contribution
I identified and analysed the stakeholders, mapped the as-is and to-be processes, and carried out the root cause analysis. I co-authored the Business Requirements Document with the team.
Team deliverables
Current-state and future-state process maps, stakeholder mapping, MoSCoW-prioritised functional requirements, non-functional requirements and a fishbone cause-and-effect diagram.
Outcome
The Business Requirements Document defines the scope, objectives, 14 stakeholders, the future-state process and the functional and non-functional requirements for an electronic medication administration system, with deployment planned for Q1 2027.
  • Business Requirements Document
  • As-is and to-be mapping
  • Root cause analysis
  • MoSCoW

Team consulting project, 2026, community health provider

Remote patient monitoring for a community health provider

The problem
Home readings for about 40,000 chronic-care patients were recorded manually and reached clinicians late, which limited visibility between appointments. The case baselines were an 18% 30-day readmission rate and 25% missed appointments.
My contribution
I carried out the stakeholder analysis, drew the as-is process flow chart, and built the Jira board, including the epics, user stories and backlog.
Team deliverables
To-be process map, options appraisal, power-interest matrix and RACI, requirements catalogue, 8 epics and 24 user stories with acceptance criteria, a three-sprint plan, and risk and privacy planning.
Outcome
The team presented a 17-slide recommendation for an integrated monitoring pathway with patient-specific alerts and EHR sync. Benefit scenarios come from the case study, are not guaranteed, and are subject to further validation.

  • Jira
  • User stories
  • Process mapping
  • RACI
